05 / PRIVACY

Your library stays yours.

PaperPulse is designed to run on your own computer. It does not turn your research history into a hosted PaperPulse account.

  • LOCAL
    Encrypted at rest

    CV files, OAuth tokens, article cache, recommendations, embeddings, and feedback are encrypted locally.

  • READ ONLY
    Inoreader stays untouched

    No automatic read, star, save, or subscription writeback.

  • TRANSPARENT
    You choose the AI provider

    Your research profile and article text are sent only to the OpenAI API you configure for ranking and analysis.

  • OPEN
    Inspect every line

    MIT licensed, public on GitHub, and built without analytics or tracking.
